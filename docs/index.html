C. The Responsive Aspirants

High Probability / Low Spending

Eager to engage but lower basket size. Likely newer customers or bargain hunters.

Strategy: Upsell & Bundle

  • Digital-first communication
  • Volume-based discounts
  • Educational content
Priority Target

A. The Loyal Elites

High Probability / High Spending

The most valuable segment. High tenure, frequent catalog users, recent buyers.

Strategy: Retain & Reward

  • Premium catalog access
  • Loyalty exclusivity
  • Personalized appreciation

D. Low-Priority Cohort

Low Probability / Low Spending

Inactive or infrequent shoppers. Often lapsed customers with high recency scores.

Strategy: Cost Reduction

  • Suppress expensive marketing
  • Automated win-back only
  • Clean from active lists

B. Disengaged Affluents

Low Probability / High Spending

High value but unresponsive. Likely in-store shoppers or resistant to direct mail.

Strategy: Nudge & Automate

  • Transactional "Thank You" offers
  • In-store/POS engagement
  • Reduce direct mail frequency
